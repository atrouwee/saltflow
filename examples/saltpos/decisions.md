# Decisions

The till's rulings, one per heading. A ruling stays ruled until a later one replaces it; the flows quote them word for word, and `saltflow validate` fails a quote that was reworded.

## Pay on leaving

Guests order, sit, order again, and are invited to pay only when they leave; the payment closes the whole visit. Tabs are the default; a quick sale is for takeaway.

## The room map

The room map is a pane from the left, opened by the map button beside +. The till recedes behind it.

## A counter sale is a short visit

A counter sale is a short visit, so one screen does both, and no guest count is asked.

## Send on leaving the tab

A round goes to the bar when the barista leaves the tab. The Send button stays for an early send.

## Ask once

Ask who the order is for once, when it first goes to the bar.

## Taps with a reason

Moves that are not a sale are taps with a reason. A void after sending is a counter-entry, never a deletion.

## Tips on the terminal

The guest picks a tip on the card terminal; it is never typed in at the till.
