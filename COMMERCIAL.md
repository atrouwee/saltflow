# Commercial use

saltFlow is free for non-commercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Charities,
schools, public research bodies and government institutions may use it whatever their funding.

Any other use, such as using saltFlow at a company, for a client, or as part of a paid product or service, needs a
commercial licence: a separate written agreement with Moonlabor. Commercial use is something we do together, so
expect a conversation about what you are building, not only a price.

## How to ask

- Open an issue titled **Commercial licence**. Say who you are, what you would use saltFlow for, and roughly how many
  people would use it. Keep anything confidential out of the issue; we will move to a private channel.

If you are not sure whether your use is commercial, ask the same way.
